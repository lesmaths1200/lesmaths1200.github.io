Test Maths 1200 on Android

Cette page en français

Before the app can go live on the Play Store, Google requires at least 12 people to test it for 14 days. Thank you for helping!

What you need
  • an Android phone or tablet (not an iPhone or iPad);
  • the Google account used on that device — the same one at every step.

The app itself is in French. That changes nothing for the test: what counts is keeping it installed.

1Join the testers group

First.

Signed in with your Google account, click “Join group”. You can choose “No email”: there is nothing to post in the group.

Join the group

groups.google.com/g/testeurs-maths1200

2Opt in to the test

Right after step 1 — on your Android device.

Tap “Become a tester”. The page then confirms: “You are a tester”.

Become a tester

play.google.com/apps/testing/ch.lesmaths1200.app

3Install the app

Right after step 2, still on your Android device.

The Play Store opens on Maths 1200: tap Install.

Open in the Play Store

play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ch.lesmaths1200.app

4For 14 days

  • Keep the app installed: uninstalling it or leaving the test costs one tester.
  • Open it now and then: a lesson, an exercise, a video, the quick quiz, a mock exam…
  • Report anything wrong (a bug, clipped text, a formula that renders badly, an idea): write to lesmaths1200@gmail.com, with a screenshot if you can.

Updates arrive on their own through the Play Store.

If something goes wrong

“Item not found” in the Play StoreCheck that the Play Store is using the same Google account as the group (your picture, top right of the Play Store). Then try again a few hours later: Google sometimes takes a while to register a new member.
You are outside SwitzerlandNot a problem: the test is open in Switzerland, France and most countries. An error message almost always comes from a different Google account than the one in the group, or from a sign-up not yet registered (see above).
“Ask to join” instead of “Join group”Click it anyway: your request will be accepted.
No ad offered to unlock a solutionNormal in the first few days: ads are being rolled out gradually.
The “Privé” tab asks for a codeIt is reserved for students of the school: you can ignore it.

The app asks for no account and no sign-up, and your progress stays on your device. Privacy policy.

Thank you very much for your help!
